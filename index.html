About

DEEVLA is an early-stage technology initiative focused on improving digital content creation workflows, particularly within Autodesk Maya and modern DCC environments.

The project explores workflow automation, artist productivity, development tools, pipeline improvement, and AI-enabled creative technologies.

The Problem

Artists and technical users often work across fragmented tools, repetitive workflows, and complex technical processes. DEEVLA aims to explore more intuitive and efficient ways of working.

Product Direction

Current areas of exploration include Autodesk Maya workflows, DCC development tools, workflow automation, asset management, AI-assisted tools, and artist-focused productivity systems.

Founder

Riyadus Solihin is a 3D professional with extensive experience using Autodesk Maya, particularly in modeling and production workflows. His interests include DCC technology, workflow innovation, artificial intelligence, and product development.

Status

DEEVLA is currently in early development and research.
